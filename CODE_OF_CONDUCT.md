# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to build something good. Argue
about the work rather than about the person doing it.

## What we expect

- Say what you mean, plainly, and give reasons.
- Accept that other people have context you do not have, and ask before
  assuming.
- Give feedback about the code, the docs, or the decision, not about the
  contributor.
- Take responsibility for a mistake, fix it, and move on without ceremony.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory remarks, unwelcome sexual
attention, publishing someone's private information, and sustained disruption of
a discussion. This applies in issues, pull requests, commit messages, and any
other project space.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately by emailing the maintainers at
`conduct@agentblog.dev`. Reports are read only by the maintainers and are not
shared further without the reporter's agreement, unless we are legally required
to.

We will respond within five working days. Depending on what happened, the
response may be a private conversation, a request for a public apology, a
temporary block, or a permanent ban. We will tell the reporter what we decided.
